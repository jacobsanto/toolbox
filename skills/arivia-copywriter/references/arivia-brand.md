## 1. Brand Overview

Arivia Villas is the consumer-facing villa rental brand of Arivia Group — a hospitality
management and technology company based in Fira, Santorini, founded in 2013.

The brand manages premium short-term rental properties across two Greek destinations:
- **Santorini** — flagship market; Cycladic architecture, caldera settings, village character
- **Athens** — urban premium properties in the city centre and downtown neighbourhoods

**Portfolio:** 30 listings (2026)
**Performance metrics:**
- 90% five-star reviews
- 52% returning guests — the single most powerful proof point in the brand
- Personal concierge service on every booking

## 2. Brand Taglines & Core Messaging

**Primary tagline:** "Live like a local with us"
**Hero headline:** "You are already here"

These two lines are the brand's north stars. Every piece of copy — regardless of format,
platform, or property — should feel consistent with both.

**"You are already here"** — The brand does not sell escape. It sells arrival.
The guest is already where they need to be. Arivia's role is to make them feel it fully.

**"Live like a local with us"** — The experiential promise. Not a tourist stay with a view,
but a genuine inhabitation of place. Insider knowledge, personal contact, real connection
to Santorini and Athens. Not a curated performance of Greece — the thing itself.

## 3. Brand Positioning

### What Arivia Is
- A premium STR brand with hotel-grade service standards and genuine local soul
- A concierge-led, personally managed experience — not self-service
- A consistent portfolio: the same quality commitment across every property
- A brand guests return to — 52% return rate is rare and significant in the STR market

### What Arivia Is Not
- A faceless aggregator or OTA
- A hotel chain with standardised rooms
- A budget or mid-market operator
- Generic luxury that could be replicated anywhere

### Positioning Statement
Arivia Villas occupies the space between boutique hotel and private villa ownership:
the intimacy and character of a privately owned property, combined with the reliability,
service standards, and local expertise of a professional hospitality brand.

## 4. Brand Voice

### The Voice in One Line
A knowledgeable local friend who happens to manage the best properties on the island —
warm, specific, trusted, never performing.

### Voice Characteristics
- **Warm intelligence** — never cold, never corporate, never over-formal
- **Earned specificity** — every claim grounded in real detail
- **Shows, never tells** — the experience revealed, not announced
- **Trusts the reader** — our guests are sophisticated; they know the difference
- **Local, not touristic** — insider perspective, not brochure language

### What the Voice Is Not
The following language has appeared in older Arivia copy and must never be reproduced:
- ❌ "breathtaking", "magnificent", "stunning" — unearned adjectives
- ❌ "cozy socializing areas" — vague and generic
- ❌ "romantic terraces that are certain to impress" — weak promise
- ❌ "paradise for couples and families" — could describe any property anywhere
- ❌ "Your dream vacation awaits" — cliché that erodes trust
- ❌ "hidden gem", "nestled", "world-class", "luxurious" — banned across all copy

**The correction:** Replace adjectives with specific details. Replace promises with pictures.
Replace generic warmth with the real thing — the concierge who knows which bakery opens at 6,
the terrace where the light hits the caldera at 7pm, the bedroom that smells of jasmine
in August.

## 5. Guest Profile

**Who books Arivia:**
- Couples, families, and small groups
- Design-conscious, experience-driven international travellers
- Age range: 30–55
- English as primary booking language; multi-national
- Motivated by authenticity and local connection — not just Instagram aesthetics

**The 52% returning guest:**
This is the most important signal in the brand. These are not guests chasing a moment —
they are guests who found something real and came back for it.

**Writing for returning guests:** Don't over-explain. Trust the relationship.
Reference the familiar without being sentimental. They know what Arivia is.

**Writing for new guests:** Earn trust through specificity. Use the 52% as social proof.
Let the detail do the convincing — not the adjectives.

## 7. Santorini Destinations

Arivia operates properties across multiple Santorini villages. Each has a distinct
character that should inform copy tone and positioning.

**Do not limit copy to only Fira, Pirgos, and Kontochori.**
The portfolio extends across the island. When a property spec sheet names a village,
use the character notes below to inform the copy.

### Fira
The capital and commercial heart of Santorini. Built on the western caldera cliffs.
Volcano views from virtually every point. The island's main shopping, dining, galleries,
and nightlife. Most connected location — everything accessible on foot or short drive.
**Copy angle:** Energy and centrality. The island's pulse. You are in the middle of everything
and above all of it at the same time.

### Firostefani
The quieter continuation of Fira along the caldera path, just north. Fewer tourists,
same caldera views, more residential feel. Walking distance to Fira.
**Copy angle:** The caldera without the crowd. Fira's neighbour, but a different pace.

### Imerovigli
The highest point on the caldera rim, known as the "balcony of Santorini." The most
dramatic elevation. Skaros Rock — the old castle ruins — is here. Extremely peaceful,
extraordinary views. Predominantly upscale properties.
**Copy angle:** Above everything. The island's highest inhabited point. Silence and altitude.

### Oia
The most iconic village. The global image of Santorini — blue domes, sunset crowds,
cave houses, Venetian sea captain architecture. Highly desirable, premium-priced.
Northwest tip of the island.
**Copy angle:** The postcard made real — but experienced from the inside, not the lookout.

### Pirgos Kallistis (Pyrgos)
The highest village on the island — inland, away from the caldera. Medieval Venetian
architecture, castle ruins at the summit, oldest settlement character. Wine country nearby
(Santorini PDO wines). Quieter, more authentic, more local.
**Copy angle:** The Santorini the tourists don't find. History, architecture, silence, wine.

### Kontochori
Traditional settlement immediately south of Fira. Quieter, more residential, authentic
village character. Short walk to Fira's centre but genuinely local atmosphere.
**Copy angle:** Village Santorini, a breath from the capital. Real life, not performance.

### Akrotiri
Southwest of the island. Archaeological site of ancient Akrotiri (Bronze Age, Minoan).
Less crowded beaches nearby — Red Beach, White Beach. Lighthouse at the tip.
More space, less tourist density, longer drives to Fira and Oia.
**Copy angle:** Ancient ground. The island before the tourists — and some of its best beaches.

### Kamari / Perissa (East Coast)
Black volcanic sand beaches. More family-oriented beach resort feel. Affordable tavernas,
beach bars, water sports. Different energy from the caldera-side villages.
**Copy angle:** The beach side of Santorini. Black sand, clear water, relaxed days.

### Megalochori
Traditional village in the south. Known for Santorini's wine production — surrounded by
vineyards. Venetian bellowers (Campaniles). Very quiet, very local.
**Copy angle:** Wine and silence. A village that still belongs to the people who live there.

Arivia operates urban properties in Athens. Specific neighbourhood details to be added

## 11. AI Image Policy
**Status: Permanent. Non-negotiable. Hardcoded into the platform.**

This policy governs all image usage across Arivia Villas — website, OTAs, social media,
email, and print. It applies to the content team, AI tools, freelancers, and any
third-party producing content on behalf of Arivia Villas.

### ✅ AI-Generated Images — PERMITTED in these contexts only:

| Context | Permitted use |
|---|---|
| Blog posts | Illustrative images, destination mood, travel context |
| Seasonal offer graphics | Campaign visuals, promotional banners |
| Campaign banners | Brand campaigns, seasonal promotions |
| Illustrative social posts | Mood content, destination storytelling, non-property posts |
| Email headers | Campaign and newsletter visuals (non-property) |

**Condition:** Even in permitted contexts, AI images must be clearly appropriate
to the content — they must not mislead the reader into believing they are viewing
a real Arivia property, room, or space.

### ❌ AI-Generated Images — STRICTLY PROHIBITED in these contexts:

| Context | Why prohibited |
|---|---|
| Property listing photos | Guest books based on these — accuracy is a legal and trust obligation |
| OTA platform images | Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, VRBO, Hotels.com, Google Hotels, Travelnest, Travelstation all prohibit misleading imagery in their terms of service |
| Any image presented as a real photo of a property | Constitutes misrepresentation |
| Room or space photography | Must reflect the actual space guests will occupy |
| Interior or exterior shots in any booking context | Real photography only |
| Guest communication imagery | Any image sent to a booked or prospective guest showing "the property" |

### Consequences of Policy Violation
- OTA listing suspension or removal (Airbnb, Booking.com and others actively detect AI imagery)
- Guest complaints and refund claims (misrepresentation)
- Brand reputation damage — the 52% return rate depends on trust
- Potential legal exposure under consumer protection regulations (EU and international)

### For the Content Team
When requesting or producing visual content:
1. Always confirm the destination context before selecting or generating an image
2. If in doubt — use real photography
3. AI image generation tools (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, etc.) are tools
   for campaign and editorial content only — never for property representation
4. Label all AI-generated images in your asset management system as "AI — editorial use only"

## 12. Arivia Circle — Loyalty Programme

### What It Is
Arivia Circle is the brand's premium returning guest programme. It formalises the
relationship Arivia already has with the 52% of guests who come back.

### Positioning
- A members club, not a points scheme
- Belonging, not transaction
- "You are already part of this" — not "earn your way in"
- Never feels like an airline miles programme — always feels like being welcomed back
  by someone who remembers your name

### Benefits
- Priority booking access
- Personalised concierge service
- Exclusive seasonal offers
- Early access to new properties

### Copy Tone for Arivia Circle
- Warm, personal, already-familiar
- Speaks to shared history — the previous stay, the return, the relationship
- Never transactional, never promotional in an aggressive sense
- The invitation is always ambient — present but never pushy
- Think: a host who remembers you left a good bottle of wine last time

## 13. New Website — Arivia Villas Content Engine

**Platform:** React + Supabase, built on Lovable
**URL:** tranquil-stays-core.lovable.app (pre-launch April 2026 → production URL TBC)
**Status:** Replacing current ariviavillas.com (Maveriks/Guesty setup)

### What the New Platform Is
Not just a new website. A content-first, AI-powered brand destination.
The shift: from property-listing site → editorial brand with booking functionality.
The brand now speaks through content depth, not just listing pages.

### Content Modules
The platform has six AI-powered content generation modules:

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Property Descriptions | Per-property, per-platform listing copy |
| Blog Content | Destination guides, seasonal articles, property stories |
| Social Media | Instagram captions, Facebook posts, campaign content |
| Email Campaigns | Welcome series, seasonal offers, Arivia Circle communications |
| Campaign Banners | Seasonal and promotional visual copy |
| Arivia Circle | Loyalty programme communications and member content |

### Brand Direction Signals
- SEO is a primary traffic strategy — pillar content, destination guides, property stories
- Each property page is a story, not a spec sheet
- The blog builds brand equity and drives organic traffic simultaneously
- Arivia Circle copy lives across the site as an ambient invitation
- Campaign copy is seasonal and alive — never evergreen boilerplate

### Copy Rules for the New Site
- **Go long where it earns it** — the platform is built for editorial depth
- **Internal linking is structural** — properties link to destinations, destinations link to blogs,
  blogs link back to properties. Copy should support this architecture.
- **Every property page has a narrative arc** — opening with feeling, closing with the booking
- **Blog content is travel-essay quality** — not listicle, not filler
- **Arivia Circle is woven in** — never a banner interruption, always a natural next step
- **Seasonal campaigns refresh regularly** — nothing sits unchanged for more than one season
