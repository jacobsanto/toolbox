# Platform-Specific Rules & Constraints

## Airbnb

**Character limits:**
- Listing title: 50 characters (hard limit)
- Description: No hard limit, but 400-600 words optimal for engagement
- Summary (first section): Most critical — shown before "Read more" fold

**Algorithm & SEO:**
- Keywords in title directly impact search ranking
- First 150 characters of description appear in preview — front-load value
- Amenity keywords (pool, view, wifi) should appear naturally in description

**Tone rules:**
- Warm and personal — Airbnb guests expect host voice, not hotel voice
- Write as if from the host: "I designed this space..." or "Guests often tell us..."
- Practical information (check-in, house rules) kept separate from marketing copy
- Avoid promotional language that sounds like an ad

**What converts on Airbnb:**
- Specificity about the neighbourhood and walking distances
- Honest description of who the space is ideal for
- Mention of small thoughtful details (coffee setup, welcome wine, etc.)

---

## Booking.com

**Character limits:**
- Property name: 60 characters
- Description: No hard limit; 250-400 words optimal
- Highlights/USPs: 3-5 bullet points (separate from main description)

**Tone rules:**
- More factual than Airbnb — Booking.com guests often comparison-shopping
- Lead with the strongest USP in sentence one
- Include star rating context and positioning if relevant
- Use third person ("The property offers...") — platform convention

**What converts on Booking.com:**
- Clear mention of cancellation/flexibility policy in copy
- Location relative to key landmarks (distances)
- Specific amenity callouts (free parking, breakfast included, etc.)

---

## Expedia / Hotels.com

**Tone rules:**
- Most transactional of the three — benefit-led, scannable
- Use short paragraphs and natural bullet integration
- Price-value framing works well here

---

## Direct Website

**No platform constraints** — this is where the brand lives fully.
- Longest copy permitted
- Most poetic, most brand-forward
- Can include full storytelling, neighbourhood guides, seasonal content
- SEO should be integrated but never at the cost of the reading experience
- Include internal links to other properties / destination content

---

## Instagram

**Post copy:**
- First line = the hook (visible before "more") — treat as a headline
- Line breaks are rhythm tools — use them intentionally
- 5-8 hashtags: mix of branded (#AriviaVillas), location (#Santorini), and niche (#LuxuryVilla)
- Best performing content: specific moments, not generic beauty

**Stories / Reels captions:**
- Shorter — 1-3 lines maximum
- More casual, present tense, immediate
- Strong CTA if applicable ("Link in bio to book")

**Hashtag strategy for luxury travel:**
Primary: #[PropertyName] #[BrandName]
Location: #Santorini #Athens #Greece #GreekIslands
Niche: #LuxuryVilla #BoutiqueTravel #SlowTravel #DesignHotel #VacationRental
Aspirational: #TravelInStyle #MediterraneanLife

---

## Facebook

- Slightly longer captions acceptable (vs. Instagram)
- More conversational, can include a question to drive comments
- Link previews compete with copy — write copy that stands alone
- Best for: offers, blog shares, community building, longer stories

---

## Google Ads

**Search ads — hard character limits (strictly enforce):**
- Headlines: 30 characters each (up to 15 headlines provided, 3 shown at once)
- Descriptions: 90 characters each (up to 4, 2 shown at once)
- Display URL path: 15 characters each field

**Performance Max / Display:**
- Headlines: 30 characters (short), 90 characters (long)
- Descriptions: 90 characters
- Provide maximum variants — Google rotates and tests

---

## Email (General)

**Subject line best practices:**
- 40-50 characters for mobile preview optimisation
- Avoid spam triggers: FREE, !!!, ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation
- Personalisation token ([First Name]) increases open rates
- Test: curiosity vs. direct benefit vs. urgency

**Preview text:**
- 40-90 characters — extends the subject line, doesn't repeat it
- Often overlooked; always write it

**Body copy:**
- Single column, mobile-first
- One primary CTA per email (not multiple competing buttons)
- CTAs: "Reserve your dates" / "Explore the property" / "Read the story"
- Never: "Click here" / "Buy now" / "Don't miss out"
