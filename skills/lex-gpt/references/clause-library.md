## How to Use This Library

These are starting-point clauses — not final legal text.
Every clause must be adapted to the specific parties, jurisdiction, and commercial context.
All clauses marked [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer
before the document is signed.

## Confidentiality Clauses

### Standard Mutual NDA Clause
```
1. CONFIDENTIALITY

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party 
("Disclosing Party") to the other ("Receiving Party"), whether orally or in writing, 
that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be 
confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.2 The Receiving Party shall: (a) hold Confidential Information in strict confidence; 
(b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written 
consent of the Disclosing Party; (c) use Confidential Information only for the purpose 
of evaluating a potential business relationship between the parties.

1.3 Obligations under this clause do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes 
publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was known to the 
Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of 
Confidential Information; (d) is required to be disclosed by law or court order.

1.4 These obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of 
[3/5] years.
```
**Annotation:** Duration of 3-5 years is standard. Perpetual confidentiality is rarely
enforceable for general business information. Trade secrets may warrant longer protection.
[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] if covering trade secrets or highly sensitive IP.

## Intellectual Property Clauses

### IP Ownership (Work for Hire)
```
INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All work product, deliverables, inventions, designs, software, documentation and 
other materials created by [Contractor/Service Provider] in connection with this 
Agreement ("Work Product") shall be considered works made for hire and shall be 
the sole and exclusive property of [Client].

To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as a work made for hire, 
[Contractor] hereby irrevocably assigns to [Client] all right, title and interest 
in and to such Work Product, including all intellectual property rights.

[Contractor] retains ownership of all pre-existing tools, methodologies, and 
know-how ("Background IP") used in performing the Services. [Contractor] grants 
[Client] a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use Background IP solely to the 
extent necessary to use the Work Product.
```
**Annotation:** The background IP carve-out is critical for contractors —
without it, a broad assignment clause could transfer ownership of their
entire toolkit. [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] for significant IP transactions.

### IP Licence (Non-assignment)
```
LICENCE GRANT

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, [Licensor] grants [Licensee] a 
[non-exclusive / exclusive], [royalty-free / royalty-bearing], [worldwide / 
territory-limited], [perpetual / term-limited] licence to use the [Software/Content/IP] 
solely for [specific permitted purpose].

This licence does not include the right to sublicence, modify, or create derivative 
works without prior written consent.
```
**Annotation:** Fill in all brackets before use. An exclusive licence restricts
the licensor from granting the same rights to others — significant commercial consequence.

## Limitation of Liability Clauses

### Standard Liability Cap
```
LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

(a) NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, 
CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, LOSS OF DATA, 
OR LOSS OF BUSINESS, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

(b) EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS 
AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL AMOUNTS PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT TO 
SERVICE PROVIDER IN THE [12] MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

(c) The limitations in this clause shall not apply to: (i) death or personal injury 
caused by negligence; (ii) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (iii) any liability 
that cannot be excluded or limited by applicable law.
```
**Annotation:** Exclusions in (c) are mandatory under most legal systems —
never remove them. The cap amount (12 months fees) is standard but negotiable.
[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] if transaction value is significant.

## Termination Clauses

### Termination for Cause
```
TERMINATION FOR CAUSE

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the 
other party: (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach 
within [30] days of written notice specifying the breach in reasonable detail; 
(b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has 
a receiver or administrator appointed; (c) ceases to conduct business in the 
ordinary course.
```

### Termination for Convenience
```
TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE

Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon [30/60/90] days' 
prior written notice to the other party. In the event of termination for convenience 
by [Client], Client shall pay Service Provider for all Services performed and 
expenses incurred up to the date of termination, plus [X]% of the remaining 
contract value as a termination fee.
```
**Annotation:** Termination for convenience is valuable but should include
appropriate compensation for the service provider — zero compensation is
often commercially unreasonable and may be challenged.

## Payment and Invoicing Clauses

### Standard Payment Terms
```
PAYMENT TERMS

(a) Client shall pay all invoices within [30] days of the invoice date.

(b) Invoices shall be submitted [monthly / on milestone completion / as agreed].

(c) Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of [8% + ECB reference rate] 
per annum from the due date until payment in full, calculated daily.

(d) Service Provider reserves the right to suspend Services if any invoice remains 
unpaid for more than [15] days after the due date, following written notice.
```
**Annotation:** The interest rate in (c) reflects the EU Late Payment Directive
(2011/7/EU) default rate. Greek law incorporates this directive.
Always check current ECB reference rate.

## Force Majeure Clause

```
FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform its obligations 
under this Agreement if such delay or failure results from circumstances beyond 
its reasonable control, including but not limited to: acts of God, natural disasters, 
pandemic, epidemic, war, terrorism, government action, industrial action, or 
failure of third-party services or infrastructure ("Force Majeure Event").

The party affected by a Force Majeure Event shall: (a) promptly notify the other 
party; (b) use reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effects; (c) resume performance 
as soon as reasonably practicable.

If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than [90] days, either party may 
terminate this Agreement on [30] days' written notice without liability.
```

## Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

### Greek Law / Greek Courts
```
GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws 
of Greece. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the 
courts of [Athens / Thessaloniki / the competent court of the place of performance].
```

### EU Jurisdiction with Arbitration
```
GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of [country].

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally 
settled by arbitration under the Rules of the [ICC / SCC / LCIA], by [one / three] 
arbitrator(s) appointed in accordance with those Rules. The seat of arbitration 
shall be [city]. The language of the arbitration shall be [English / Greek].
```
**Annotation:** [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] for choice of arbitration institution
and rules — selection has significant procedural and cost implications.

## GDPR Data Protection Clauses

### Controller-to-Processor Clause
```
DATA PROTECTION

(a) For the purposes of this clause, "Personal Data", "Data Subject", "Controller", 
"Processor", and "Processing" have the meanings given in GDPR (EU 2016/679).

(b) The parties agree that [Client] is the Data Controller and [Service Provider] 
is the Data Processor in respect of Personal Data processed under this Agreement.

(c) Service Provider shall: (i) process Personal Data only on documented instructions 
from Client; (ii) ensure persons authorised to process Personal Data are bound by 
confidentiality obligations; (iii) implement appropriate technical and organisational 
security measures; (iv) assist Client in responding to Data Subject rights requests; 
(v) delete or return all Personal Data on termination; (vi) make available to Client 
all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this clause.

(d) Service Provider shall not transfer Personal Data outside the EEA without 
Client's prior written consent and appropriate transfer mechanisms in place.
```
**Annotation:** A full Data Processing Agreement (DPA) should be executed
as a separate document for significant data processing relationships.
See GDPR compliance reference for full DPA template.

## Employment Clauses (Greek Law)

### Probationary Period
```
PROBATIONARY PERIOD

The first [12] months of employment constitute a probationary period, during which 
either party may terminate the employment relationship without notice or compensation, 
subject to applicable law. Notwithstanding the above, the provisions of Law 4808/2021 
regarding unjustified dismissal apply from the commencement of employment.
```
**Annotation:** Greek Law 4808/2021 introduced anti-discrimination protections
from day one. Probationary termination must not be discriminatory.
[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] for all employment contracts.

### Non-Competition (Greek Law)
```
NON-COMPETITION

Employee agrees that during the term of employment and for a period of [12] months 
following termination, Employee shall not, within [geographic scope], directly or 
indirectly engage in activities that compete with the business of Employer, 
provided that Employer pays Employee [X]% of their last monthly salary for each 
month of the non-compete period.
```
**Annotation:** Under Greek law, post-employment non-compete clauses require
compensation to be enforceable. Uncompensated non-competes are generally void.
Maximum reasonable scope: 1-2 years, relevant geographic area.
[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] — enforcement depends on specific facts.
