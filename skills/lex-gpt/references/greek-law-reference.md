## Greek Civil Code — Contract Fundamentals

### Contract Formation
A valid contract under Greek law requires:
1. **Agreement:** Offer and acceptance (Articles 185-200 AK)
2. **Capacity:** Parties must have legal capacity (Articles 127-136 AK)
3. **Lawful purpose:** Object must not be contrary to law, public order, or good morals
4. **Possible performance:** The contracted obligation must be possible

### Formality Requirements (Article 158 AK)
**General rule:** Contracts are valid without written form unless law specifically requires it.

**Exceptions — written form required by law:**
- Real estate purchase/sale: notarial deed required
- Employment contracts (certain types): written form required
- Some insurance contracts
- Mortgage and pledge agreements

**Practical note:** Even when not legally required, written contracts are strongly
advisable for commercial relationships — they prevent disputes about terms.

### Void vs Voidable Contracts
**Void (μηδενικές):** Invalid from inception, cannot be ratified.
Causes: Illegal purpose, contrary to public order, incapacity of minors.

**Voidable (ακυρώσιμες):** Valid until challenged within limitation period.
Causes: Error, fraud, duress, undue influence.

### Force Majeure (Ανωτέρα Βία)
Article 336 AK: A debtor is released from liability for non-performance
if performance became impossible due to circumstances not attributable to them.
The event must be: unforeseeable, unavoidable, and external.

COVID-19 was accepted as force majeure by Greek courts in specific contexts
— but each case depends on its specific circumstances.

## Greek Employment Law

### Key Legislation
- **Law 4808/2021:** Current employment law framework (major reform)
- **Law 3863/2010:** Core labour market rules
- **Presidential Decree 80/2022:** Working time regulation
- **Greek Civil Code Articles 648-680:** Employment contract basics

### Employment Contract Types

**Indefinite term (Αορίστου Χρόνου):**
Standard employment relationship. Most protections apply.
Cannot terminate without cause or severance payment.

**Fixed-term (Ορισμένου Χρόνου):**
Maximum 3 years total (with renewals).
Cannot be used to circumvent indefinite term protections.
Automatic conversion to indefinite if exceeded or repeatedly renewed.

**Part-time:**
Must be in writing. Must specify hours. Pay proportional to full-time.

### Probationary Period
Law 4808/2021: Up to 12 months probationary period allowed.
During probation: employer can terminate without notice or severance.
**Exception:** Cannot terminate for discriminatory reasons even during probation.

### Notice Periods for Indefinite Contracts

| Service length | Notice required |
|---|---|
| Up to 1 year | 1 month |
| 1-2 years | 2 months |
| 2-5 years | 3 months |
| 5-10 years | 4 months |
| 10+ years | Up to 6 months |

In lieu of notice: pay equivalent to notice period.

### Severance Payment (Αποζημίωση Απόλυσης)

| Service length | Severance |
|---|---|
| 1-4 years | 2 months salary |
| 4-6 years | 3 months salary |
| 6-8 years | 4 months salary |
| 8-10 years | 5 months salary |
| 10+ years | 6 months salary (white collar maximum reduced) |

**Note:** Reduced severance applies if full notice period is given.
These amounts are based on standard monthly salary (not including benefits).

### Working Hours
Standard: 8 hours/day, 40 hours/week.
Overtime above 9 hours/day: 120% of hourly rate.
Sunday work: 175% of daily rate.
Night work (10pm-6am): 25% premium.

### ΕΦΚΑ (Social Security) Obligations
Employee contribution: approximately 13.87% of gross salary
Employer contribution: approximately 22.29% of gross salary
Registration with ΕΦΚΑ required before first day of employment.
Monthly declarations and payments required.

### Leave Entitlements
Annual leave: Minimum 20 working days (increases with seniority).
Sick leave: Paid by employer for up to 3 days, then ΕΦΚΑ.
Maternity leave: 17 weeks paid.
Paternity leave: 14 days paid.

## Greek Business Entity Types

### ΙΚΕ (Ιδιωτική Κεφαλαιουχική Εταιρεία)
**Introduced by:** Law 4072/2012
**Minimum capital:** €1 (nominal)
**Liability:** Limited to capital
**Governance:** Manager(s) — no board required for small companies
**Best for:** Startups, SMEs, professional services, foreign investors
**Registration:** ΓΕΜΗ (General Commercial Registry)
**Accounting:** Full double-entry bookkeeping required

### ΕΠΕ (Εταιρεία Περιορισμένης Ευθύνης)
**Minimum capital:** €4,500
**Liability:** Limited to capital
**Governance:** Manager(s) and assembly of partners
**Older structure:** ΙΚΕ now preferred for most new companies

### ΑΕ (Ανώνυμη Εταιρεία)
**Minimum capital:** €25,000 (paid up)
**Liability:** Limited to share capital
**Governance:** Board of Directors, General Assembly
**Best for:** Larger companies, companies seeking investment, listed companies
**Reporting:** More extensive — annual financial statements published

### ΟΕ (Ομόρρυθμη Εταιρεία) / ΕΕ (Ετερόρρυθμη Εταιρεία)
**Unlimited liability** for at least one partner (ΟΕ: all partners; ΕΕ: general partners)
**Rarely recommended** for new commercial ventures
**Still used** for certain family businesses and simple partnerships

## Greek Consumer Law

### Law 2251/1994 (as amended)
Core consumer protection framework.

**B2C contract requirements:**
- Clear and understandable language
- Pre-contractual information obligations
- Unfair terms are null and void
- Examples of unfair terms: unreasonable limitations of liability,
  unilateral amendment rights, automatic renewal without adequate notice

### Distance Selling (E-commerce)
**Right of withdrawal:** 14 calendar days from delivery (goods) or conclusion (services)
**Exceptions:** Custom goods, perishables, digital content (once started with consent)
**Refund:** Within 14 days of withdrawal notification

### Law 4933/2022 — Updated Consumer Protections
Implements EU Omnibus Directive.
Price display requirements for promotions.
Enhanced penalties for unfair commercial practices.

## Greek Courts and Dispute Resolution

### Court System
**Ειρηνοδικείο (Justice of the Peace):** Claims up to €20,000
**Πρωτοδικείο (Court of First Instance):** Claims above €20,000
**Εφετείο (Court of Appeal):** Appeals from first instance
**Άρειος Πάγος (Supreme Court):** Final court of cassation

### Limitation Periods
**General contract claims:** 20 years (Article 249 AK)
**Commercial claims:** 5 years
**Employment claims:** 5 years from termination
**Consumer claims:** 2 years for product defects
**Tort:** 5 years from knowledge of damage and responsible party

### Mediation (Law 4640/2019)
Mandatory mediation attempt before certain civil litigation.
Court-annexed mediation available.
Private commercial mediation available.
Can be faster and cheaper than litigation for commercial disputes.

### Arbitration
Available for commercial disputes.
Hellenic Chamber of Commerce and Industry offers arbitration services.
ICC arbitration commonly used for international commercial disputes.
