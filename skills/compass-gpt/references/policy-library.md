## AI Usage Policy

```
AI USAGE POLICY
Version: 1.0 | Effective: [Date] | Owner: [IT/Operations/HR]
Next review: [Date + 12 months]

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs the use of artificial intelligence tools by [Company] employees,
contractors, and third parties acting on behalf of [Company]. It ensures AI tools
are used responsibly, securely, and in compliance with applicable law.

2. SCOPE
Applies to: All employees, contractors, and third parties using [Company] systems
or acting on [Company]'s behalf.

3. APPROVED AI TOOLS
Employees may use the following AI tools for work purposes:
[List approved tools]

Use of AI tools not on the approved list requires prior written approval from [role].

4. PROHIBITED INPUTS
The following data must NOT be entered into any AI tool unless specifically approved
in writing by [role]:

4.1 Personal data of employees, customers, or third parties (names, emails,
    identification numbers, health data, or any data covered by GDPR)
4.2 Client confidential information
4.3 Trade secrets, proprietary processes, or unreleased product information
4.4 Financial data (unpublished results, forecasts, pricing)
4.5 Legal advice or privilege-protected communications
4.6 Passwords, access credentials, or security information

5. OUTPUT VERIFICATION
5.1 AI-generated content must be reviewed and verified by a human before being
    sent to clients, published, or relied upon for decisions.
5.2 Employees are responsible for the accuracy of any AI-generated content
    they use or submit.
5.3 AI tools must not be used as the sole basis for decisions affecting individuals
    (performance assessments, hiring, disciplinary matters).

6. DISCLOSURE AND ATTRIBUTION
6.1 Employees must disclose AI assistance when:
    - Submitting work to clients as original analysis
    - Publishing content under [Company]'s name
    - Submitting academic or professional materials
6.2 The extent of disclosure required for internal work is [define: encouraged / not required].

7. DATA RESIDENCY AND SECURITY
7.1 Before using any approved AI tool, employees must verify that the tool's data
    processing location complies with [Company]'s data residency requirements.
7.2 AI tools that process data outside the EU require prior DPO/legal approval.

8. ENFORCEMENT
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination, in accordance with [Company]'s Disciplinary Procedure.
[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW]

9. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed annually or when significant AI regulatory changes occur.
```

## Remote / Hybrid Work Policy

```
REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK POLICY
Version: 1.0 | Effective: [Date] | Owner: HR
[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW] — Greek Law 4808/2021 applies

1. PURPOSE
To define the conditions under which [Company] employees may work remotely or
in a hybrid arrangement, and to ensure productivity, security, and wellbeing.

2. SCOPE
Applies to: All employees whose roles are eligible for remote/hybrid work.
Not all roles are eligible — eligibility is determined by job function and
management discretion.

3. ELIGIBILITY
Remote or hybrid work may be offered to employees who:
3.1 Have completed [probationary period / 6 months / as agreed]
3.2 Hold roles that can be performed effectively outside the office
3.3 Have demonstrated consistent performance
3.4 Have a suitable working environment at home

4. AGREEMENT
4.1 Remote/hybrid work requires a written agreement specifying:
    - Days and hours of remote work
    - Equipment provision arrangements
    - Availability and communication expectations
    [REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW — Law 4808/2021 requires written agreement]

5. EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES
5.1 [Company] will provide: [list equipment — laptop, headset, etc.]
5.2 Employees are responsible for: [internet connection / as agreed]
5.3 Reimbursement for eligible home-working expenses: [define or reference expense policy]

6. AVAILABILITY AND COMMUNICATION
6.1 Remote employees must be available during core hours: [define hours]
6.2 Response time expectations: [e.g., within 2 hours during core hours]
6.3 Employees must participate in all required meetings regardless of location

7. RIGHT TO DISCONNECT
[Company] respects employees' right to disconnect outside working hours.
Employees are not expected to respond to non-urgent communications outside
their agreed working hours. [Law 4808/2021]

8. DATA SECURITY
8.1 Employees must use [Company]-approved VPN when accessing company systems remotely
8.2 Confidential materials must not be left unattended
8.3 Company devices must not be used by other household members
8.4 Employees must lock screens when leaving devices unattended

9. HEALTH AND SAFETY
Employees working from home are responsible for ensuring their home workspace is safe.
[Company] will provide a home working assessment checklist.

10. PERFORMANCE AND OVERSIGHT
Remote work does not change performance expectations.
Employees on remote/hybrid arrangements are subject to the same performance
management processes as office-based employees.

11. REVIEW
Remote/hybrid arrangements may be reviewed or withdrawn:
- If performance declines
- If business needs change
- At the annual review of the arrangement
```

## Code of Conduct

```
CODE OF CONDUCT
Version: 1.0 | Effective: [Date] | Owner: HR/Management
[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW]

1. PURPOSE
This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour [Company] expects from
all employees, contractors, and representatives. It reflects our values and our
commitment to a respectful, ethical, and professional workplace.

2. SCOPE
Applies to: All employees, contractors, consultants, and anyone acting on behalf
of [Company] — including conduct at company events and on social media when
representing [Company].

3. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOUR
3.1 Treat all colleagues, clients, partners, and third parties with respect and dignity.
3.2 Maintain a professional standard in all communications, written and verbal.
3.3 Represent [Company] honestly and accurately in all external interactions.

4. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
4.1 Employees must disclose any personal interest that could conflict with
    their professional responsibilities to [Company].
4.2 Disclosure must be made to [direct manager / HR] in writing.
4.3 Employees must not allow personal interests to influence business decisions.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
5.1 Employees must protect [Company]'s confidential information at all times.
5.2 Confidential information must not be shared with third parties without
    authorisation, during or after employment.
5.3 This obligation survives termination of employment.

6. USE OF COMPANY RESOURCES
6.1 Company systems, equipment, and resources are for business use.
6.2 Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work
    or create security, legal, or reputational risk.
6.3 Employees have no expectation of privacy on company systems.
    [REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW — monitoring must comply with GDPR and Greek law]

7. ANTI-BRIBERY AND CORRUPTION
7.1 [Company] does not tolerate bribery in any form.
7.2 Employees must not offer, give, request, or accept anything of value
    to improperly influence a business decision.
7.3 Gifts and hospitality above [€[amount]] must be disclosed to [role].

8. RESPECT AND DIGNITY
8.1 Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind is prohibited.
8.2 See [Harassment and Anti-Discrimination Policy] for full details.
    [Law 4808/2021 — mandatory for companies with 20+ employees]

9. REPORTING VIOLATIONS
9.1 Employees who become aware of a violation of this Code must report it to
    [HR / direct manager / whistleblower channel].
9.2 Reports will be treated with confidentiality to the extent possible.
9.3 Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.

10. ENFORCEMENT
Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination. Serious violations may be referred to legal authorities.
[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW]
```

## Data Protection / GDPR Internal Policy

```
INTERNAL DATA PROTECTION POLICY
Version: 1.0 | Effective: [Date] | Owner: DPO / HR / Management
[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW]

1. PURPOSE
To ensure [Company] handles all personal data in compliance with GDPR
(EU 2016/679) and applicable national data protection law, and to protect
the rights of individuals whose data we process.

2. SCOPE
Applies to: All employees, contractors, and third parties who process personal
data on behalf of [Company].

3. DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES
All personal data must be:
3.1 Processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently
3.2 Collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
3.3 Limited to what is necessary for the purpose
3.4 Accurate and kept up to date
3.5 Kept for no longer than necessary
3.6 Protected by appropriate security measures

4. LAWFUL BASIS FOR PROCESSING
Before processing personal data, employees must confirm there is a lawful basis:
- Contract: necessary for a contract with the individual
- Legal obligation: required by law
- Legitimate interest: [Company]'s documented legitimate interest
- Consent: freely given, specific, informed consent

5. EMPLOYEE DATA
[Company] processes employee data for employment management purposes.
Employees have the right to access their own data — contact [HR/DPO].
Full employee privacy notice is at: [location]

6. DATA SECURITY
6.1 Personal data must not be sent via unsecured channels
6.2 Access to personal data is restricted to those with a business need
6.3 Laptops and devices must be password-protected and encrypted
6.4 Physical documents containing personal data must be stored securely

7. DATA BREACH RESPONSE
If you suspect a personal data breach:
7.1 Report to [DPO/IT/Manager] immediately — within the hour
7.2 Do not attempt to resolve it yourself
7.3 The DPO will assess and notify the Hellenic DPA within 72 hours if required

8. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
Employees must refer any data subject request (access, deletion, correction)
to [DPO/HR] within 24 hours of receipt.
Response deadline: 1 month from receipt.

9. THIRD-PARTY PROCESSORS
Before sharing personal data with any third party, confirm:
9.1 A Data Processing Agreement is in place
9.2 The third party has adequate security measures
9.3 No transfer outside EEA without appropriate safeguards
```
