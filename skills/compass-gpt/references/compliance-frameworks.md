## GDPR for HR — Full Compliance Framework

### Employee Data Inventory

Every HR data processing activity must be documented in the Records of Processing Activities (ROPA):

| Activity | Data categories | Lawful basis | Retention | Processors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruitment | CV, ID, references | Legitimate interest / Consent | 6 months post-rejection | ATS provider |
| Employment contract | ID, bank, tax, salary | Contract + Legal obligation | Duration + 5 years | EFKA systems |
| Payroll | Salary, tax, IBAN | Legal obligation | 10 years (tax law) | Payroll provider |
| Performance management | Assessment records | Legitimate interest | Duration + 1 year | HR system |
| Health/sick leave | Health data (special) | Legal obligation | Duration + 5 years | Insurance provider |
| Disciplinary records | Incident records | Legitimate interest | Duration + 3 years | HR system only |
| Monitoring | Access logs, email meta | Legitimate interest | 90 days | IT systems |

**Special category data (health, biometric, etc.):**
Requires explicit consent OR falling within specific Art. 9(2) exemptions.
Health data for sick leave: legal obligation basis.
[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW] for any special category processing.

### Employee Privacy Notice Requirements

Must be provided at the point of data collection (recruitment stage at latest).
Must include:
- Identity and contact details of the controller
- Contact details of DPO (if appointed)
- Purposes and legal basis for each processing activity
- Retention periods
- Recipients and international transfers
- Employee rights and how to exercise them
- Right to complain to Hellenic DPA (www.dpa.gr)

### Monitoring in the Workplace (Greek Law)

Greek law and GDPR impose strict limits on employee monitoring.

**Permitted (with conditions):**
- Network access logs for security purposes
- CCTV in common areas (not private areas) — with notice
- Company email monitoring — with advance notice in employment contract
- GPS tracking for company vehicles — with legitimate purpose and notice

**Not permitted:**
- Continuous keystroke logging
- Screenshot monitoring without specific justification and notice
- Monitoring of personal devices even on company network
- Covert monitoring (except in very specific criminal investigation contexts)

**Requirement:** Any monitoring policy must be:
- Disclosed to employees in writing (employment contract or policy)
- Proportionate to the legitimate purpose
- Reviewed for GDPR compliance

## EU Whistleblowing Directive — Implementation Framework

### Greek Implementation: Law 4990/2022

**Scope:** Companies with 50+ employees.
50-249 employees: Implementation required from December 2023.
250+ employees: Already required.

**Required elements:**

1. **Internal reporting channel**
   - Confidential or anonymous option
   - Accessible to all employees (and optionally: contractors, suppliers)
   - Can be: dedicated email, web portal, designated person, third-party provider
   - Must be secure — IT cannot have access to anonymous reports

2. **Designated person or team**
   - Receives and manages reports
   - Must be independent (HR Director, Compliance Officer, Legal)
   - Can be outsourced to third-party provider

3. **Acknowledgement**
   - Within 7 days of receiving a report

4. **Follow-up**
   - Within 3 months: inform reporter of action taken or planned

5. **Non-retaliation protection**
   - Reporters protected from: dismissal, demotion, harassment, any detriment
   - Retaliation is itself a legal violation
   - Protection also extends to: colleagues supporting the reporter, legal entities
     associated with the reporter

6. **Confidentiality**
   - Reporter identity must not be disclosed without consent
   - Exception: legal proceedings require disclosure

**Reporting categories covered:**
- Violations of EU law (the Directive's scope)
- In Greece: Law 4990/2022 extends to violations of national law as well

### Whistleblowing Policy — Required Clauses

```
WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION POLICY
[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW — Law 4990/2022]

1. Purpose and commitment to non-retaliation
2. Who can report (employees, contractors, suppliers, former employees)
3. What can be reported (scope of reportable conduct)
4. How to report:
   - Internal channel: [description, URL, or contact]
   - Anonymous option: [yes/no and how]
   - External channels: Hellenic Authority for Public Administration
     and relevant regulatory authorities
5. What happens after a report:
   - Acknowledgement: within 7 days
   - Investigation process
   - Feedback: within 3 months
6. Confidentiality protections
7. Non-retaliation protections (specific — not vague)
8. Consequences for false reports in bad faith
9. Record-keeping and GDPR compliance
```

## EU AI Act — Internal Policy Framework

### Risk-Based Compliance Approach

Before drafting an AI policy, inventory all AI tools in use:

| AI Tool | Function | Risk category | Policy requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Tool 1] | [Use] | Minimal | Basic use guidelines |
| [Tool 2] | [Use] | Limited | Transparency requirements |
| [Tool 3] | [Use] | High | Full compliance programme |
| [Tool 4] | [Use] | Unacceptable | Prohibited — do not use |

**Minimal risk (most tools):** ChatGPT for content, Copilot for coding.
Basic guidelines sufficient. No specific regulatory obligation beyond GDPR.

**Limited risk:** Chatbots interacting with customers.
Must disclose the system is AI (not human).

**High risk:** AI in HR decisions, credit scoring, education.
Significant compliance obligations — risk management, transparency, human oversight.

**Unacceptable risk:** Social scoring, manipulation, most real-time biometric.
Prohibited under the AI Act. Do not use.

### AI Policy Compliance Checklist

- [ ] AI tool inventory completed and risk-categorised
- [ ] Prohibited use cases identified and communicated
- [ ] Data protection review: what personal data flows through AI tools?
- [ ] DPAs in place with AI providers processing personal data
- [ ] Employee training on AI limitations and responsible use
- [ ] Human oversight defined for AI-assisted decisions
- [ ] Disclosure obligations met (customer-facing AI identified as AI)
- [ ] High-risk AI: conformity documentation in place [REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW]

**January:**
- [ ] Update employment contracts for any legislative changes from prior year
- [ ] Review salary levels against minimum wage (if applicable)
- [ ] Issue updated privacy notice if any processing has changed

**April:**
- [ ] Review leave entitlements (annual leave accrual)
- [ ] Update any policies affected by Q1 legislative changes

**June:**
- [ ] Prepare for summer holiday period — leave management
- [ ] Review working hours and overtime compliance

**October:**
- [ ] Annual policy review cycle begins
- [ ] Update employee handbook for any changes

**December:**
- [ ] Ensure all ΕΦΚΑ payments current
- [ ] Christmas bonus (Δώρο Χριστουγέννων) compliance — 
  due by December 21st: one full month's salary for indefinite contracts
- [ ] Annual review completion

### Mandatory Bonuses — Greek Law

**Christmas bonus (Δώρο Χριστουγέννων):**
1 full month's salary for employees working full year.
Pro-rated for partial year.
Due: on or before December 21st.

**Easter bonus (Δώρο Πάσχα):**
Half month's salary.
Due: by Holy Wednesday (before Easter).

**Leave allowance (Επίδομα Αδείας):**
Half month's salary OR the amount of the leave taken — whichever is lower.
Paid when employee takes annual leave.

**Failure to pay mandatory bonuses:** Labour inspectorate violation.

## Policy Communication Framework

### Deployment Checklist for New or Updated Policies

**Before launch:**
- [ ] Legal review completed (where required)
- [ ] Management approval obtained
- [ ] Version number and effective date set
- [ ] Previous version archived

**Launch:**
- [ ] Published to [HR system / Notion / intranet / shared drive]
- [ ] All affected employees notified (email + confirmation of receipt)
- [ ] Manager briefing conducted if policy involves management discretion
- [ ] Training provided if policy requires new employee behaviour

**Post-launch:**
- [ ] Acknowledgement tracking: record who has confirmed receipt
- [ ] Q&A process: designated contact for policy questions
- [ ] Follow-up for non-acknowledgers: reminder at 2 weeks and 4 weeks

**Acknowledgement record (minimum for key policies):**
Employee name | Policy title | Version | Date acknowledged | Method
[required for: data protection, code of conduct, harassment, AI usage policy]
