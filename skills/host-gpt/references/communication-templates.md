# Communication Templates
# HostGPT Reference — Messages by stage, industry, and channel

---

## Template Usage Rules

Every template has personalisation slots marked with [BRACKETS].
Never send a template without filling every slot.
If a slot cannot be filled — rewrite the sentence rather than leaving it generic.

Tone calibration: adjust every template to match brand tier.
These are written at premium level — scale up or down as needed.

---

## Pre-Arrival Sequence (Hospitality / Villa / Hotel)

### T-7 Days: Anticipation + Practical Info + Soft Upsell

Subject: Your stay at [Property Name] — everything you need to know

Dear [Guest Name],

We are looking forward to welcoming you to [Property Name] on [Arrival Date].

Your [room/villa/suite] will be ready from [check-in time]. [One sentence about
what makes their specific accommodation special — terrace, view, private pool, etc.]

A few things worth knowing before you arrive:
- Address and parking: [details]
- Check-in: [time] — [process, e.g., "we'll meet you at the property"]
- WiFi: [network/password] — already set up and waiting
- [One local tip specific to arrival date/season]

[OPTIONAL UPSELL — only if relevant to their stay type]
Should you wish to arrive to something special, we can arrange [flowers/wine/
dinner reservation/airport transfer] — simply reply to this message by [date]
and we'll take care of it.

If you have any questions before your arrival, [name] is available at [contact].

With warm regards,
[Name]
[Property Name]

[Property Name]

## Complaint Acknowledgement Templates

### Immediate Acknowledgement (under 2 hours)

"Dear [Name], thank you for reaching out. We've received your message and
[Name] will be in contact within [timeframe] to resolve this personally.
We take this seriously. — [Your name], [Property/Company]"

### Follow-Up When Resolution Will Take Time

Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your patience. I wanted to update you personally.

I have investigated [specific issue] and [what I found / what is happening].
[Specific action being taken] will be complete by [specific time].

I will confirm once resolved. If you need to reach me directly in the
meantime: [direct contact].

Again, I apologise sincerely for [specific thing]. This is not the experience
we want for our guests/clients.

[Name]
[Title]
