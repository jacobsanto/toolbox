## EU Procurement Framework

### TED (Tenders Electronic Daily)

ted.europa.eu — the EU's official source for public tenders above EU thresholds.

**Published here:**
- All Greek public contracts above EU thresholds
- EU institution contracts
- Contracts from all 27 EU member states
- EEA countries (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein)

**How to monitor:**
- Set up email alerts by CPV code and/or country
- Filter by contracting authority type
- Check award notices to understand market pricing

**Submission:**
For EU-threshold Greek tenders: submit via ΕΣΗΔΗΣ (not directly on TED).
TED notice is for awareness only — ΕΣΗΔΗΣ is the submission platform.

## EU Direct Grants and Tenders

### European Commission Procurement (ETENDERING/eTENDERS)

The European Commission and EU agencies procure directly.
Platform: etendering.ec.europa.eu

**Common EU institution contracts:**
- Framework contracts for IT services
- Research and analysis services
- Communication and translation
- Consultancy

**Characteristics:**
- English-language (primarily)
- Very high quality standards
- Long evaluation timelines (4-12 months)
- Strong competition from large consultancies
- Entry point: apply for cascade contracts and framework positions first

### EU Agency Tenders

Each EU agency (EMA, EIB, EASA, etc.) has its own procurement.
Start with smaller contracts to build track record.

## ΕΣΠΑ (European Structural and Investment Funds)

ΕΣΠΑ is the Greek implementation of EU Structural Funds.
Currently: ΕΣΠΑ 2021-2027

### How ΕΣΠΑ Works

**Two procurement streams:**

**Stream 1 — Direct grants to beneficiaries:**
Companies and organisations apply for grants to implement projects.
The grant recipient then runs their own procurement for suppliers.
Entry point for suppliers: respond to tenders issued by ΕΣΠΑ beneficiaries.

**Stream 2 — Public procurement by managing authorities:**
Managing authorities (ministries, regions) procure directly using ΕΣΠΑ funds.
Normal Greek procurement rules apply (N.4412/2016) but with ΕΣΠΑ co-financing.

### ΕΣΠΑ Strategic Positioning

**For service providers:**
1. Monitor ΕΣΠΑ managing authority procurement on ΕΣΗΔΗΣ
   (filter by ΕΣΠΑ-funded contracts)
2. Build relationships with frequent ΕΣΠΑ beneficiaries in your sector
3. Track open calls for grant applications — some allow consortium participation

**ΕΣΠΑ-funded contracts characteristics:**
- Often stricter documentation requirements (EU audit trail)
- Frequent reporting obligations
- Audit risk: all ΕΣΠΑ-funded contracts subject to EU audit
- Payment terms can be slower (EU verification adds time)

**Key ΕΣΠΑ 2021-2027 priority areas:**
- Digital transformation
- Green economy and climate
- Innovation and research
- Social inclusion
- Regional development

## Horizon Europe

Horizon is the EU's research and innovation funding programme (2021-2027).
Budget: €95.5 billion.

### Relevance for B2G Strategy

Horizon is not public procurement — it is competitive grant funding.
But it is highly relevant for technology and innovation companies as:
1. Direct path to EU funding for R&D activities
2. Builds international consortium experience
3. Creates EU-level visibility that enhances public sector credibility

### Horizon Instrument Types

**EIC (European Innovation Council):**
For startups and SMEs with breakthrough innovations.
- EIC Accelerator: up to €17.5M (grant + equity)
- EIC Pathfinder: early-stage research
SME-friendly — direct application, no consortium required.

**Research and Innovation Actions (RIA):**
Collaborative research projects with universities/institutions.
Requires consortium of 3+ organisations from 3+ EU countries.

**Innovation Actions (IA):**
Closer to market than RIA. More commercial partners.

### Application Approach

Horizon proposals are evaluated by independent expert reviewers on:
- Excellence (the idea)
- Impact (the difference it makes)
- Implementation (can this team do it?)

Greek organisations regularly participate — especially in digital,
health, and sustainability themes.

Entry strategy: join an existing consortium first before leading one.
Connect with: Greek Horizon contact points (EYDE Horizon), research universities.

## World Bank and Multilateral Development Banks

### World Bank Procurement

Platform: devbusiness.worldbank.org

**Types of contracts:**
- Consulting services
- Goods and works
- Non-consulting services

**Key features:**
- International competitive bidding
- English-language documentation
- QCBS (Quality and Cost-Based Selection) common for consulting
- Procurement notice period: 4-6 weeks minimum

**Entry strategy:**
1. Register on World Bank procurement platform
2. Search for contracts in your sector/geography
3. Build track record through subcontracting to larger firms first
4. Develop World Bank-format CVs and company profile

### European Investment Bank (EIB)

eib.org/en/projects/procurement

EIB-financed projects use borrower procurement — typically following national
or EU rules, with EIB oversight. Monitor Greek public entity procurement
for EIB co-financed projects.

## International Procurement — Strategy Summary

| Framework | Entry difficulty | Contract size | Key differentiation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greek N.4412/2016 | Low-medium | €5K–€50M | Relationship, local presence |
| EU institution direct | High | €50K–€5M | Quality, multilingual, EU knowledge |
| ΕΣΠΑ-funded | Medium | €50K–€2M | ΕΣΠΑ experience, documentation rigour |
| Horizon grants | High | €500K–€17.5M | Innovation, research credentials |
| World Bank | High | $200K–$10M | International experience, specific sector |

**Recommended sequencing for a new entrant:**
1. Win Greek direct awards (build track record)
2. Win Greek open tenders (build competitive credibility)
3. Pursue ΕΣΠΑ-funded contracts (build EU compliance experience)
4. Apply for EU-funded projects as consortium member
5. Build toward direct EU institution or international bids

## ΚΗΜΔΗΣ — Public Contracts Intelligence

ΚΗΜΔΗΣ (Κεντρικό Ηλεκτρονικό Μητρώο Δημοσίων Συμβάσεων) is the Greek
central register of all public contracts.

**Strategic use:**
- Research what contracts a specific authority has awarded previously
- Find award prices for similar contracts
- Identify which competitors win work in your target sector
- Monitor payment performance of contracting authorities
- Access: www.eprocurement.gov.gr

**What you can find:**
- Contract award notices (all values)
- Contract modification notices
- Payment completion certificates
- Tender cancellations

This is market intelligence freely available — use it before every tender.
