## N.4412/2016 — Framework Overview

Greece's primary public procurement law implementing EU Directives 2014/24/EU
(public sector) and 2014/25/EU (utilities).

Key principles (Article 18):
- Equal treatment and non-discrimination
- Transparency
- Proportionality
- Mutual recognition (EU)

## Open Procedure (Article 27) — Step by Step

### Phase 1: Pre-Tender (Internal to Contracting Authority)

1. Needs assessment and market research
2. Specification drafting (τεχνικές προδιαγραφές)
3. Internal approval (budget, legal, departmental)
4. Entry on ΚΗΜΔΗΣ (Central Register of Public Contracts)

**Strategic window for suppliers:** This is when relationships matter most.
If you know the tender is being prepared, engage now — not after publication.

### Phase 2: Publication

1. Contract notice published on ΕΣΗΔΗΣ
2. Simultaneous publication in ΚΗΜΔΗΣ
3. For EU-threshold contracts: also in OJEU/TED (Official Journal EU)
4. Publication includes: specifications, technical requirements, evaluation criteria

### Phase 3: Submission Period

**Standard minimum:** 35 calendar days from publication
**Accelerated (justified urgency):** 15 days minimum
**Extension:** May be granted if significant specification changes

**What suppliers submit:**
- Technical offer (Τεχνική Προσφορά)
- Financial offer (Οικονομική Προσφορά) — in sealed envelope
- Qualification documents (Δικαιολογητικά Συμμετοχής)
- All via ΕΣΗΔΗΣ platform (electronic submission)

### Phase 4: Evaluation

1. **Opening session:** Committee opens envelopes publicly (or electronically)
2. **Qualification check:** Does each bidder meet minimum criteria?
3. **Technical evaluation:** Scoring of technical offers against criteria
4. **Financial opening:** Only qualifying technical offers proceed to financial opening
5. **Combined score:** Technical score × weight + Price score × weight
6. **Ranking and recommendation:** Committee recommends winner

**Price score calculation (common formula):**
```
Price Score = (Lowest offer / Bidder's offer) × 100 × price_weight
```

**Typical evaluation timeline:** 4-12 weeks depending on complexity and committee availability.

### Phase 5: Award and Challenge

1. **Preliminary notification:** All bidders notified of intended award
2. **Standstill period:** 10 days (mandatory — allows challenges)
3. **Challenge window:** Unsuccessful bidders may challenge via ΑΕΠΠ
4. **Award decision:** If no valid challenge — contract awarded
5. **ΚΗΜΔΗΣ notification:** Award published

### Phase 6: Contract Signature

1. Winning bidder submits full qualification documents
2. Performance guarantee (εγγύηση καλής εκτέλεσης): typically 4-5% of contract value
3. Contract signed — starts the clock on deliverable deadlines

**Typical timeline from publication to signature: 3-6 months**

## ΕΣΗΔΗΣ Platform Guide

ΕΣΗΔΗΣ (Εθνικό Σύστημα Ηλεκτρονικών Δημοσίων Συμβάσεων) is the mandatory
electronic procurement platform for all public contracts in Greece.

**Registration:**
Suppliers must register at www.promitheus.gov.gr before submitting to any tender.
Registration requires: ΑΦΜ, ΓΕΜΗ number, legal representative details.
Allow 5-7 business days for account activation.

**Submission process:**
- All documents submitted electronically through ΕΣΗΔΗΣ
- Electronic signature required (qualified electronic signature recommended)
- Financial offer submitted in separate encrypted file
- Submission confirmation email is proof of timely submission

**Key ΕΣΗΔΗΣ deadlines:**
- Must be registered BEFORE the submission deadline
- Technical issues with the platform are not accepted as submission failure excuse
- Submit at least 24 hours before deadline — platform can be slow under load

**Monitoring:**
ΕΣΗΔΗΣ allows searching published tenders by:
- CPV code
- Contracting authority
- Publication date range
- Contract value range

## Light Regime (Articles 107-110 + Annex XIV)

Applies to specific service categories listed in Annex XIV of N.4412/2016.

**Key Annex XIV categories:**
- Health and social services (CPV 85000000-9 and related)
- Educational services (CPV 80000000-4 and related)
- Legal services (CPV 79100000-5 and related)
- Certain administrative services
- IT consultancy services (selected CPV codes)

**Different rules from standard procedure:**
- Publication still required, but simplified notice
- Minimum time limits shorter
- More flexibility in evaluation criteria
- Specific requirements may vary by category

**[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW]** for:
- Confirming whether specific CPV codes fall under Annex XIV
- Drafting the required Legal Note (Νομικό Σημείωμα)
- Confirming threshold applicability

## Qualification Document Checklist

Standard documents required in most Greek public tenders:

**For Legal Entities (ΑΕ, ΕΠΕ, ΙΚΕ, etc.):**
- [ ] Criminal record certificate (ποινικό μητρώο) — for legal representative(s)
- [ ] Tax clearance certificate (φορολογική ενημερότητα) — AADE, max 3 months old
- [ ] Social security clearance (ασφαλιστική ενημερότητα) — ΕΦΚΑ, max 3 months old
- [ ] ΓΕΜΗ certificate (ΠΔ/GE.MI.) — current, max 6 months old
- [ ] Articles of association + any amendments
- [ ] Authorisation letter (if not legal representative signing)
- [ ] Financial statements (last 2-3 fiscal years — certified)
- [ ] Bank reference letter (if required for financial capacity)
- [ ] Reference contracts list (similar projects, last 3-5 years)

**For Consortia (Κοινοπραξία):**
- All above for each member
- Consortium agreement (signed before submission or conditional)
- Lead member designation

**For ΙΚΕ specifically:**
- Same as general legal entity requirements
- Ensure ΚΑΔ codes match contract subject
- Ensure ΕΦΚΑ obligations current

## Challenge and Appeal Process (ΑΕΠΠ)

ΑΕΠΠ (Ενιαία Αρχή Δημοσίων Συμβάσεων) is the procurement oversight authority.

**Challenge timeline:**
- Challenge must be filed within 10 days of preliminary award notification
- Filing fee required (varies by contract value) — refunded if challenge succeeds
- ΑΕΠΠ decision: typically within 20-30 working days

**Grounds for challenge:**
- Specification allegedly discriminatory
- Evaluation procedure violated
- Qualification documents incorrectly assessed
- Price calculation error

**Strategic consideration:**
Filing a challenge delays award by 30-60 days minimum.
Before filing: assess whether the grounds are solid and whether
the relationship with the contracting authority can survive the challenge.
A failed challenge that delays a contract creates lasting hostility.

## B2G Relationship Development Framework

### The Pre-Tender Intelligence Sequence

The most valuable procurement intelligence is gathered before the tender is published.

**Information to gather pre-tender:**
- What is the contracting authority's current strategic plan?
  (Check their annual operational plan — often public)
- What contracts are expiring in the next 12 months?
  (Check ΚΗΜΔΗΣ for existing contracts and their end dates)
- What budget is available? (Annual budget documents are public)
- Who owns the procurement decision?
  (Director General / CFO / IT Director — depends on contract type)
- What problems has the authority reported publicly?
  (Annual reports, parliamentary questions, audit reports)

### Stakeholder Mapping Template

For each target contracting authority:

```
Authority: [Name]
ΑΦΜ/Code: [Code]
Primary contact: [Name, Title, Contact]
Decision authority: [Who approves contracts of this value]
Champion (if any): [Internal advocate — name, title]
Influencer(s): [Others who shape the decision]
Current vendor(s): [Existing suppliers in relevant category]
Contract expiry: [Date of relevant existing contracts]
Budget cycle: [When annual budget is approved]
Key pain points: [Documented or observed]
Relationship status: None / Introductory / Warm / Active
Last interaction: [Date and nature]
Next action: [Specific, dated]
```

### Entry Points by Authority Type

**Hospital / Healthcare entity:**
Technical director or IT director for digital solutions.
Finance director for accounting/financial systems.
Medical director for clinical process solutions.
Entry: attend healthcare sector events, professional associations,
       respond to market consultations.

**Municipality:**
Mayor's office for strategic partnerships.
Technical services for infrastructure.
Finance for financial systems.
Entry: local business associations, municipal council meetings,
       attend public sessions where strategy is discussed.

**Ministry:**
General Secretary (Γενικός Γραμματέας) for senior-level engagement.
Department heads for specific initiatives.
Entry: sector association engagement, policy consultation participation,
       invitation-only briefings.

**Public Hospital Group / Central Authority:**
Board-level for strategic decisions.
Operational leadership for delivery contracts.
Entry: professional medical/healthcare associations, sector publications.

### The Relationship-First Principle Applied

**What to do 6-12 months before a tender:**
1. Request an informational meeting — not a sales meeting
   "We work with similar organisations and are interested in understanding
   your current priorities — not to pitch anything, but to understand
   whether our work is relevant to your needs."

2. Demonstrate sector knowledge
   Share a relevant case study, research finding, or market insight
   (free, no strings) that genuinely helps them.

3. Offer to participate in market consultation
   Many contracting authorities run pre-tender market consultations.
   Participate formally and thoughtfully.

4. Stay visible without being intrusive
   One meaningful contact every 6-8 weeks maximum.
   Quality of contact matters more than frequency.

**What NOT to do:**
- Push for a meeting when there is no genuine value to offer yet
- Reference competitor contracts in a way that sounds like disparagement
- Make commitments you cannot keep
- Send generic marketing materials
- Follow up so frequently that you become a nuisance

The relationship must exist on its own merits — not as a procurement tactic.
Contracting authority officials recognise when interest is purely transactional.

## Route Selection — Strategic Profile

The procurement route determines everything: timeline, competition, risk, and
the relationship dynamic with the contracting authority.

**Route 1: Απευθείας Ανάθεση (Direct Award) — Article 118**
```
Threshold: Up to €30,000 excl. VAT (goods/services)
           Up to €60,000 excl. VAT (works)
[VERIFY current thresholds — subject to periodic revision]

Process:
- Single or limited quotation process
- No public notice required
- Contracting authority approaches known suppliers
- Decision by authorised official — no committee required
- Timeline: 1-4 weeks

Strategic implications:
- Relationship with contracting authority is decisive
- First mover advantage is significant
- Specification is often written with known supplier in mind
- Entry requires demonstrating capability before need arises

Risk level: Low — if relationship is established
            High — if cold approach to unknown authority
```

**Route 2: Light Regime — Articles 107-110 + Annex XIV**
```
Threshold: Typically €30,000–€221,000 excl. VAT (services)
[VERIFY current thresholds]

Applies to: Social services, health services, educational services,
            legal services, certain IT consultancy services
CPV codes listed in Annex XIV of N.4412/2016

Process:
- Public notice required (simplified)
- Minimum time limits shorter than standard open procedure
- Evaluation criteria: quality/price ratio
- Legal Note (Νομικό Σημείωμα) may be required confirming eligibility

Strategic implications:
- More flexible than full open procedure
- Relationship still matters significantly
- Technical offer quality is primary differentiator
- Single decision-maker (if authorised) or small committee

Risk level: Medium — requires compliance confirmation
```

**Route 3: Ανοικτός Διαγωνισμός (Open Procedure) — Article 27**
```
Threshold: Above EU thresholds
[VERIFY current EU thresholds — revised every 2 years]

Process:
- Full public notice on ΕΣΗΔΗΣ and ΚΗΜΔΗΣ
- Minimum submission period: 35 days (standard)
                              15 days (urgent — justified)
- Evaluation committee: minimum 3 members
- Award criteria must be stated in advance
- Challenge and appeal rights (ΑΕΠΠ)

Strategic implications:
- Prior relationship is less decisive but still valuable
- Technical offer quality and price competitiveness both critical
- Full transparency — all offers evaluated and ranked publicly
- Timeline: 3-6 months from publication to contract award

Risk level: High investment of time/resources upfront
            Legal exposure if procedure is challenged
```

**Route 4: Framework Agreement**
```
Used by central purchasing bodies (e.g., ΓΓΠΣ, ΚΗΜΔΗΣ catalogues)
Multiple suppliers pre-qualified
Individual call-offs under agreed terms

Strategic implications:
- Get on the framework first — then individual contracts flow
- Less competition per call-off
- Relationship with framework manager matters
```
