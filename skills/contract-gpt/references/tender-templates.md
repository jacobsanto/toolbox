## Deliverable Table Templates

### Standard 4-Deliverable Structure (Direct Award / Light Regime)

| Παραδοτέο | Περιγραφή | Προθεσμία | Αντίτιμο % | Κριτήρια Αποδοχής |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Π1 | [Description] | Ημ.1 + 30 ημ. | 25% | Γραπτή έγκριση [Role] |
| Π2 | [Description] | Ημ.1 + 90 ημ. | 25% | [Specific criteria] |
| Π3 | [Description] | Ημ.1 + 120 ημ. | 25% | [Specific criteria] |
| Π4 | [Description] | Ημ.1 + 150 ημ. | 25% | Τελική έγκριση |

### 6-Deliverable Structure (More Complex / Higher Value)

| Παραδοτέο | Περιγραφή | Προθεσμία | Αντίτιμο % | Κριτήρια Αποδοχής |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Π1 | Inception report + work plan | Day 30 | 15% | Approved work plan |
| Π2 | [Substantive deliverable] | Day 60 | 20% | [Measurable criteria] |
| Π3 | [Substantive deliverable] | Day 90 | 20% | [Measurable criteria] |
| Π4 | [Substantive deliverable] | Day 120 | 20% | [Measurable criteria] |
| Π5 | [Monthly/ongoing deliverable] | Monthly | 15% | [Per-period criteria] |
| Π6 | Final report + handover | Day 150 | 10% | Written final acceptance |

**Payment structure notes:**
- First payment (Π1) ideally within 30-45 days of signature
- Avoids complete upfront payment
- Avoids back-loading all payment in final deliverable
- Monthly deliverable (Π5) appropriate for ongoing advisory engagements

## Qualification Self-Assessment Checklist

Before entering any public tender, verify these are in order:

### Legal Standing (Δικαιολογητικά Νομιμότητας)
- [ ] ποινικό μητρώο — criminal record certificate, max 3 months
      (For legal representative AND all board members in ΑΕ/ΕΠΕ)
- [ ] φορολογική ενημερότητα — tax clearance, max 3 months
      Obtain from: myAADE portal → Certificates
- [ ] ασφαλιστική ενημερότητα — social security clearance, max 3 months
      Obtain from: efka.gov.gr portal
- [ ] ΓΕΜΗ certificate — max 6 months old
      Obtain from: gemi.gr

### Financial Capacity (Οικονομική Επάρκεια)
- [ ] Financial statements last 2-3 years (certified by accountant)
- [ ] Annual turnover ≥ 2× contract value (check specification)
- [ ] Bank reference letter (if required by specifications)

### Technical Capacity (Τεχνική Ικανότητα)
- [ ] List of reference contracts (similar scope, last 3-5 years)
- [ ] Certificates of satisfactory completion from each reference
- [ ] Key personnel CVs (match specification requirements)
- [ ] Professional certifications (if mandated by specifications)

### ΙΚΕ Specific
- [ ] ΚΑΔ codes include contract subject activity
- [ ] ΕΦΚΑ contributions current
- [ ] ΓΕΜΗ registration active and address current
- [ ] Contract to define deliverables, NOT attendance/presence

## Financial Offer Strategy

### Price Calculation Principles

**Cost-plus approach:**
1. Calculate real cost of delivery (team time × rates + expenses)
2. Add overhead allocation (typically 15-25%)
3. Add profit margin (typically 10-20% for public sector)
4. Check against market range for similar contracts

**Competitive positioning:**
- Research similar contracts on ΚΗΜΔΗΣ (award values are public)
- Position within 10-15% of expected market price
- Underpricing signals inability to deliver
- Overpricing requires strong technical differentiation

**VAT treatment:**
- Most public services: standard VAT 24%
- Some educational/medical services: reduced or exempt
- [VERIFY WITH ACCOUNTANT] for specific VAT treatment

### Financial Offer Format

Most Greek public tenders require a specific financial offer format.
Follow the contracting authority's template exactly.
Common format:

```
ΟΙΚΟΝΟΜΙΚΗ ΠΡΟΣΦΟΡΑ / FINANCIAL OFFER

Αξία χωρίς ΦΠΑ / Value excl. VAT:    € [X,XXX.XX]
ΦΠΑ [24%]:                             € [X,XXX.XX]
Συνολική αξία / Total value incl. VAT: € [X,XXX.XX]

[Breakdown by deliverable if required by specifications]

Ισχύς προσφοράς / Offer validity: [180 days — or as specified]
```

## Post-Award Contract Management

### First Steps After Award

1. **Performance guarantee:** Submit within 10 days of award notification
   (εγγύηση καλής εκτέλεσης — typically 4-5% of contract value excl. VAT)
   Obtain from bank or insurance company

2. **Contract signing:** Attend signing at contracting authority
   Bring: original documents, authorised signatory

3. **Kick-off meeting:** Request within 7-10 days of signature
   Confirm: contact persons, deliverable timeline, reporting format

4. **Day 1 actions:** Begin Π1 immediately
   Document all communications in writing from day 1

### Deliverable Submission Protocol

For each deliverable:
1. Submit in writing (registered mail or ΕΣΗΔΗΣ platform)
2. Request written acknowledgement of receipt
3. Acceptance period: typically 15-30 days (check contract)
4. If not accepted within period: follow up formally in writing
5. Invoice ONLY after written acceptance received

### Variation / Change Management

If scope needs to change after contract signature:
- Changes must be formally agreed and documented
- Supplementary agreement (συμπληρωματική σύμβαση) required for material changes
- No verbal agreements — everything in writing
- Major changes may require new procurement procedure
- [LEGAL REVIEW — LexGPT] for any contract variation

## Technical Offer Structure — Short Form

```
ΤΕΧΝΙΚΗ ΠΡΟΣΦΟΡΑ / TECHNICAL OFFER
[Contract Title] | [Contracting Authority]
[Date] | [Reference Number]

SECTION A: UNDERSTANDING OF THE REQUIREMENT
1.1 Our understanding of the contracting authority's needs
1.2 The specific challenges we have identified
1.3 Our interpretation of the required deliverables

SECTION B: PROPOSED METHODOLOGY
2.1 Approach overview
2.2 Phase 1: [Title] — [Scope and timeline]
2.3 Phase 2: [Title] — [Scope and timeline]
2.4 Phase N: [Title] — [Scope and timeline]
2.5 Risk management approach

SECTION C: DELIVERABLES AND MILESTONES
3.1 Deliverable schedule (table format)
3.2 Acceptance criteria per deliverable
3.3 Reporting obligations

SECTION D: TEAM
4.1 Key personnel and roles
4.2 CVs (as required by specifications)
4.3 Subcontractors (if applicable)

SECTION E: QUALITY ASSURANCE
5.1 Internal review process
5.2 Issue escalation procedure

SECTION F: RELEVANT EXPERIENCE
6.1 Reference projects (table: entity, scope, value, outcome)
6.2 Letters of reference (attached where available)

SECTION G: ADDED VALUE
7.1 Elements beyond the minimum specification
7.2 Innovation or efficiency gains offered
```
