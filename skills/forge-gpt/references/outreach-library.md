# ForgeGPT Reference — Templates by channel and context

## The Golden Outreach Rules

Before any template — the rules that govern all outreach:

1. **Answer why me, why now, why you** — in that order
2. **Under 150 words** — every word must earn its place
3. **One ask** — not "let me know if interested" — a specific, low-friction next step
4. **No attachments on first contact** — they create friction and trigger spam filters
5. **Personalise the opening** — the first sentence must be specific to them
6. **One piece of social proof** — not a list, one relevant reference
7. **Never beg** — desperation is detectable and repellent

## Cold Email Templates

### Template 1 — Trigger Event (New Role)
Subject: [Company] + [Your company name]

Hi [First name],

Saw you recently joined [Company] as [Title] — congratulations.

Leaders coming into [their role] at [company size] companies often find [specific pain point] 
becomes a priority in the first 90 days.

We help [ICP description] [specific outcome]. [One customer] went from [before] to [after] 
in [timeframe].

Worth a 20-minute call this week to see if there's a fit?

[Your name]

### Template 2 — Trigger Event (Funding Round)
Subject: Congrats on the raise — quick question

Hi [First name],

Congrats on the [Series X] — strong signal of where [Company] is headed.

Companies at your stage typically [specific challenge that follows funding].
We help [ICP] solve [specific problem] — [Customer] did [specific result] with us 
after their Series [X].

15 minutes to explore if timing is right?

[Your name]

### Template 3 — Problem-Led (No Trigger Event)
Subject: [Specific pain point for their industry]

Hi [First name],

[Specific observation about their company/industry that shows research].

Most [their role] at [company type] tell us [specific pain point] takes up 
[time/resource] they'd rather spend on [higher-value activity].

We solve this for [similar company]. [Specific result].

Open to a quick call [day] or [day] this week?

[Your name]

### Template 4 — Mutual Connection (Warm)
Subject: [Mutual connection] suggested I reach out

Hi [First name],

[Mutual connection] mentioned you're working on [relevant context] — 
thought it was worth a direct introduction.

We help [ICP] [specific outcome]. We worked with [relevant reference customer] 
on [specific thing] — [result].

Would a 20-minute call make sense? Happy to work around your schedule.

[Your name]

### Template 5 — Follow-Up (No Response)
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [First name],

Following up on my note from [X days ago].

Totally understand if the timing isn't right — just wanted to make sure 
my message didn't get buried.

[One-line value restatement]. 

Worth a quick call?

[Your name]

### Template 6 — Re-engagement (Cold Lead)
Subject: Still relevant?

Hi [First name],

We spoke [timeframe] ago about [topic]. Things have likely moved on at 
[Company] — thought it was worth checking in.

We've since [relevant update about your company/product].

If [specific trigger relevant to them], happy to reconnect.

[Your name]

## LinkedIn Outreach Templates

### Connection Request Note (300 character limit)
"Hi [Name] — noticed [specific observation about their work/post/company].
Working on something similar in [your context]. Would value connecting."

### Follow-Up After Connection
"Thanks for connecting, [Name].

Noticed [specific thing about their work] — we help [ICP] with [specific problem].
[One relevant result].

Worth 15 minutes to see if there's a fit?"

### Post-Engagement Message (They liked/commented on your content)
"Thanks for engaging with my post on [topic], [Name].

Given your work at [Company], thought [specific relevant insight] might be relevant.

Happy to share more context if useful — worth a quick chat?"

## A/B Subject Line Library

### For new relationship outreach:
A: "[Company] + [Your company]"
B: "Quick question for [their role]"
C: "[Mutual contact] suggested I reach out"
D: "[Specific trigger event reference]"

### For follow-up:
A: "Re: [original subject]"
B: "Still relevant?"
C: "Following up — [one-line value]"

### For re-engagement:
A: "Has anything changed at [Company]?"
B: "Worth reconnecting?"
C: "[Timeframe] ago we spoke about [topic]"

### What never to use:
- "Quick question" (overused, feels clickbait)
- "Touching base" (no value signal)
- "I wanted to reach out" (obvious and weak)
- Excessive punctuation: "URGENT!!!" or "Must read!"
- All caps: "OPPORTUNITY FOR [COMPANY]"
