### Scoring Guide

**Industry match:**
5 = Primary ICP industry
3 = Adjacent industry, can work
1 = Outside target industry

**Company size:**
5 = Perfect size range
3 = Slightly outside range, may still work
1 = Well outside target size

**Trigger event:**
5 = Strong trigger present (funding, new hire, regulatory)
3 = Mild trigger (industry trend affecting them)
1 = No identified trigger

**Prioritisation:**
80-100 points: Immediate priority — high-touch outreach
60-79 points: Secondary priority — standard outreach sequence
Under 60 points: Low priority — nurture only or deprioritise

## Partnership Agreement Key Terms

Every partnership agreement — even informal ones — should address:

**Scope:**
- What exactly is each party committing to do?
- What is explicitly NOT included?
- What geographies or segments are covered?

**Commercial terms:**
- Referral fee structure (% of revenue, fixed fee, barter)
- Payment timing (on signature, on payment, on milestone)
- Minimum commitment (if any)
- Exclusivity (is this exclusive? In what territory/segment?)

**Accountability:**
- How will referrals be tracked?
- Who owns the customer relationship post-referral?
- What happens if a referred deal is lost?

**Brand and representation:**
- How can each party use the other's brand?
- Who approves joint marketing materials?
- What happens if one party acts in a way that damages the other's reputation?

**Duration and exit:**
- Initial term (minimum 6-12 months to allow the partnership to produce results)
- Renewal terms
- Exit clause (how to exit without legal dispute)
- What happens to in-progress deals if the partnership ends?

## Deal Structure Frameworks

### Referral Agreement Structure

**Simple referral fee:**
10-20% of first year contract value
Paid within 30 days of customer payment
No ongoing royalty

**Ongoing revenue share:**
5-15% of ongoing contract value for the life of the customer
Better for SaaS and subscription models
More complex to administer

**Barter (reciprocal referrals):**
No cash — both parties refer to each other
Works when deal values are roughly equivalent
Define what constitutes a "qualified referral" upfront

---

### Reseller Agreement Structure

**Margin structure:**
Typical: 15-30% margin for resellers
Higher margin for exclusivity or volume commitment
Lower margin for non-exclusive, low-volume arrangements

**Support obligations:**
Who provides customer support — reseller or vendor?
What training is provided to the reseller?
What marketing support does the vendor provide?

**Minimum commitment:**
Revenue minimum (quarterly or annual) to maintain reseller status
If minimums not met: non-exclusive, or relationship ends

---

### Co-Marketing Agreement Structure

**Content ownership:**
Each party retains rights to their own contribution
Joint content owned jointly — specify usage rights
Logo usage: mutual approval required

**Distribution:**
Both parties commit to specific distribution actions:
"X will send to [Y] email subscribers by [date]"
"Y will post [Z] times on LinkedIn"
Vague commitments produce vague results — be specific

**Lead handling:**
Who receives leads generated from joint activity?
How are leads split if both parties contributed?
Lead follow-up timeline (no lead left uncontacted for 48+ hours)

## Pipeline Management Templates

### Weekly Pipeline Review Template

For each active opportunity, review:

1. **Last meaningful two-way communication:** [Date]
2. **Current stage:** [Stage]
3. **Next agreed action:** [Action] by [Who] by [Date]
4. **Deal value:** [Amount]
5. **Probability:** [%]
6. **Expected close:** [Date]
7. **Primary risk:** [What could kill this deal?]
8. **Action required this week:** [Specific action]

**Rule:** Any deal with no two-way communication in 30+ days
and no agreed next action is NOT active pipeline. Mark as "nurture" or remove.

## BD Calendar — Key Timing Intelligence

### Universal BD Timing Rules

**Best days to send outreach:**
Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday — in that order
Monday is catch-up day. Friday is wind-down day.

**Best times:**
7-9am (before meetings start) or 4-6pm (after afternoon meetings)
Avoid: 12-2pm (lunch), After 6pm (feels intrusive)

**Follow-up timing:**
First follow-up: 3-4 business days after first contact
Second follow-up: 5-7 business days after first follow-up
Third follow-up: 2 weeks after second
After three unanswered contacts: move to nurture, re-engage in 60-90 days

### Greek Market Calendar

Avoid for outreach (businesses effectively closed or distracted):
- August 1-20: Summer shutdown (many businesses closed)
- August 15: Assumption of Mary — national holiday, non-negotiable
- Holy Week (variable, Orthodox calendar): Wednesday-Sunday before Easter
- Easter Sunday + Monday: Greece takes both days
- Christmas: December 24 - January 2 (extended)
- Clean Monday (Καθαρά Δευτέρα): Start of Lent — variable, quiet period
- October 28 (Ohi Day): National holiday
- March 25 (Independence Day): National holiday

### EU Market Calendar

Key periods to avoid:
- August: Much of Southern Europe slows significantly
- Christmas/New Year: December 23 - January 3 (most of EU)
- Easter: Variable — Northern Europe less affected, Southern more so

### US Market Calendar

Key periods to avoid:
- Thanksgiving week (4th Thursday November)
- Christmas/New Year: December 24 - January 2
- July 4th week: Summer slowdown begins
- Labor Day week (1st Monday September): Summer officially ends, refocus period

**Best prospecting periods (US):**
- January-March: New budget year, new goals, high receptivity
- September-October: Post-summer reset, Q4 planning
